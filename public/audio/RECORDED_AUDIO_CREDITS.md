# Recorded audio

All source assets below are released under CC0 1.0. Source downloads are retained by their authors; this repository ships compressed MP3 derivatives.

- **Kenney — Casino Audio**: https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips
  `foley-card-slide-{1,2,3}.mp3`, `foley-card-shuffle.mp3`: recorded card handling, loudness adjusted and encoded to MP3. No large pitch shifts.
- **HydroGene — JRPG Epic Rock Battle Theme #1**: https://opengameart.org/content/jrpg-epic-rock-battle-theme-1
  `battle-rock.mp3`: loop, encoded at 96 kbps; `result-victory.mp3`: 12-second excerpt with fade.
- **RandomMind — Medieval: Exploration**: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-exploration
  `menu-fantasy.mp3`: 32-second excerpt with fades; `result-defeat.mp3`: slowed and low-pass-filtered excerpt with fade.

Music uses the independent music gain control, not the SFX gain. Menu/shop use the exploration excerpt; battles use the rock loop; victory/defeat switch to one-shot result cues. The oscillator sequencer has been removed.
